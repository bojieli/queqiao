A block that sealed short because the producer stopped is now sized to a
delivery probability rather than to throughput. The throughput objective prices
a retransmission in symbols the flow could have sent instead, and a flow that
just stopped was not going to send them, so it declined the parity and took the
round trip. On a token stream over the China-US path that left a 1.38% residual
after coding, and every unrepaired symbol is a reader watching a sentence stop.
